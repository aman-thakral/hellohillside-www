How do I…?

How do I add things?

Type, say, or snap a photo into the Log on Home — “out of milk,” “taco night Thursday” — and Hillside files each one where it belongs. Back from the store? Drop in the receipt and the whole shop is logged at once.

How do I add a paper receipt?

You don't need a special scanner — your iPhone reads the text right off the photo. Open the Camera (or Photos) and frame the receipt; a little Live Text icon appears in the corner. Tap it, then Select All and Copy — iOS pulls every line off the paper for you. Now open the Log in Hillside and paste, and the whole shop is filed at once. No Live Text icon? Touch and hold any word on the receipt instead, drag to select all of it, and copy.

How do I use it with Claude?

In Hillside, open your household settings and tap Generate token under “Claude connector” — it produces a connector URL. In the Claude app, go to Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector, paste that URL, and save. Now just ask — “what's on the list?”, “we're out of butter.” Claude reads and updates your household for you, by voice or text.

How do I get it as an app on my iPhone?

Hillside is a web app, but it installs like a native one. In Safari, open app.hellohillside.com, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. You'll get a Hillside icon that opens full-screen — no browser bars, with pull-to-refresh. (On Android, use Add to Home screen from Chrome's ⋮ menu.)

What's a household — and how do I add my family?

A household is the shared space everything lives in: the shopping list, your notes and to-dos, and the activity history belong to it, not to one person — and each member sees the same data on their own device. To add someone, open Settings → Members, tap Generate invite, and send them the link (good for 7 days); they join your household and set their own password.
