How do I…?

How do I get it as an app on my iPhone?

Hillside is a web app, but it installs like a native one — a Home-Screen icon that opens full-screen, no browser bars, with pull-to-refresh:

  1. In Safari, open app.hellohillside.com.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

On Android, use Add to Home screen from Chrome's ⋮ menu.

How do I add things?

Type, say, or paste into the Log on Home — “out of milk,” “taco night Thursday” — and Hillside files each one where it belongs. Back from the store? Drop in the receipt and the whole shop is logged at once.

How do I update my inventory from a receipt?

Drop in a receipt and the whole shop is marked as in stock at once — your list reflects what's actually home, no ticking off item by item. No scanner needed; your iPhone reads the text right off the photo:

  1. In Camera or Photos, frame the receipt.
  2. Tap the Live Text icon in the corner.
  3. Hit Select All, then Copy.
  4. Open the Log in Hillside and paste.

No Live Text icon? Touch and hold any word on the receipt, drag to select the rest, and copy.

How do I use it with Claude?

Connect Hillside to Claude once, then just ask — by voice or text — and it reads and updates your household for you:

  1. In Hillside, open your household settings.
  2. Under “Claude connector,” tap Generate token to get a connector URL.
  3. In the Claude app, go to Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  4. Paste the URL and save.

Now just ask — “what's on the list?”, “we're out of butter.”

What's a household — and how do I add my family?

A household is the shared space everything lives in: the shopping list, your notes and to-dos, and the activity history belong to it, not to one person — and each member sees the same data on their own device. To add someone:

  1. Open Settings → Members.
  2. Tap Generate invite.
  3. Send them the link (good for 7 days).

They join your household and set their own password.
