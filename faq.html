Hillside Log in

Hillside is one shared place for everything your household keeps track of — groceries, plans, the gate code, the small things. Say it any way you like, type or speak, and Hillside files each one where it belongs. See what it does →

How do I…?

How do I get it as an app on my iPhone?

Hillside is a web app, but it installs like a native one — a Home-Screen icon that opens full-screen, no browser bars, with pull-to-refresh:

  1. In Safari, open app.hellohillside.com.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

On Android, use Add to Home screen from Chrome's ⋮ menu.

How do I add things?

Type, say, or paste into the Log on Home — “out of milk,” “taco night Thursday” — and Hillside files each one where it belongs. Back from the store? Snap the receipt right there and the whole shop is logged at once.

How do I add to a list I already keep?

Got a note you keep coming back to — a kids' reading list, gift ideas, restaurants to try? You don't have to open it. Say the thing in the Log and Hillside finds the matching list and drops it on the end, tidied into a clean line.

  1. Make a note and give it a clear title — “Kids' reading list,” “Gift ideas,” “Restaurants to try.”
  2. Later, just say it in the Log — “add The Wind in the Willows to the kids' reading list.”
  3. Hillside spots your reading list and adds it to the end, cleaned up — no opening the note, no scrolling to the bottom.

The list has to already exist — Hillside adds to one you keep, it won't invent one from a stray line.

How do I stock the list when I'm just starting out?

Don't add things one at a time — brain-dump everything you've already got. Open the Log and say or type it all in one go: “rice, pasta, olive oil, a dozen eggs, milk, the good coffee, two onions…” Hillside splits the rundown into separate items and stocks your list, so you're set up in one sitting instead of ticking off a hundred boxes.

How do I update my inventory from a receipt?

Snap the receipt right in the Log and the whole shop is marked as in stock at once — your list reflects what's actually home, no ticking off item by item. No scanner, no copy-paste: Hillside reads the photo on the spot.

  1. Open the Log on Home.
  2. Tap Take Photo to snap the receipt, or Choose Photo to pick one from your library — you can also paste or drag a photo in.
  3. Hillside reads it into the box and the button turns into Log receipt ↑.
  4. Glance over the items it pulled out, untick anything you're not keeping, and tap Log receipt ↑.

Don't worry if the scanned text looks messy in the box — that's just the raw read, and Hillside tidies it into a clean, checkable list before anything is logged. The photo is kept with the entry, so you can open it later and see where each item came from. Prefer text? Pasting the lines straight in still works too.

How do I use it with Claude?

Connect Hillside to Claude once, then just ask — by voice or text — and it reads and updates your household for you:

  1. In Hillside, open your household settings.
  2. Under “Claude connector,” tap Generate token to get a connector URL.
  3. In the Claude app, go to Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  4. Paste the URL and save.

Now just ask — “what's on the list?”, “we're out of butter.”

How do I add my family?

Your family shares one household — the shopping list, notes, to-dos and history live in it, and everyone sees the same thing on their own device. To add someone:

  1. Open Settings → Members.
  2. Tap Generate invite.
  3. Send them the link (good for 7 days).

They join your household and set their own password.
